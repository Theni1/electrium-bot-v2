## Description

A long-range electric road bike designed from the ground up to give members hands-on engineering experience with a personal mobility vehicle. The goal was to ride to London, Ontario on a single charge.

## Goals

Through this project, the team gained experience in mechanical design, FEA, manufacturing, firmware, and PCB design.

## Features

### Custom Frame

The bike is built on a 25CrMo4 chromoly frame designed for durability and performance. It was one of Electrium's first frames built entirely from scratch (cut, notched, and welded). Members researched road bike geometry, translated it into CAD models, selected tubing, and created a full assembly while maintaining proper geometry. The bike also has a custom-bent 4130 steel sheet downtube, shaped to house the modular battery pack and integrating a secure locking mechanism.

### Electrical System

A 36V modular battery pack (10S6P, 20A) and a 500W mid-drive motor balance energy density and discharge capability for long-range performance. A custom anti-spark system was also developed for testing and integration.

### Custom Bike Computer

An onboard computer tracks speed, distance, and GPS data, displaying real-time metrics on a high-contrast LCD screen. Riders interact with it through a compact custom PCB in a 3D-printed casing.
