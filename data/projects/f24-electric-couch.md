## Description

The Speed Sofa is an electric-powered sofa. The team designed and built a custom vehicle frame, electrical components, and firmware, then mounted a couch on top.

## Features

### Mechanical Frame

The sofa chassis was designed and manufactured from 1-inch thick steel bars to be cost-effective and weight-optimized. It features ergonomic braking and a fork steering mechanism. The Speed Sofa reached speeds of 20 km/h while maintaining structural integrity.

### Electrical System

A 48V battery pack powers an in-hub motor.

### Display and Controls

An embedded display shows speed and battery level. The team was working on CAN communication between the ESP32 and the display to increase reliability.
