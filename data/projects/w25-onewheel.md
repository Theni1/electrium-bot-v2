## Description

A Onewheel is a self-balancing electric skateboard with a single large wheel in the center, using sensors and gyroscopes to stay stable. Riders control speed and direction by leaning forward or backward, like snowboarding or surfing on land. Electrium's Onewheel has a custom-made frame, a 60V electrical system, and dynamic underglow lighting.

## Goals

The team aimed to improve their firmware, electrical, and mechanical design knowledge. Members gained experience with SolidWorks weldments, electrical schematics, and configuring PID control on the VESC. They learned about Battery Management Systems to support the custom 16S2P battery pack, as well as woodworking and machining the frame's structural components.
