## Description

A fully electric motorized bike, built as a sustainable, energy-efficient, and accessible alternative to a car. It has a thumb throttle for acceleration and a touch screen display for speed, distance, and battery tracking. Built by the Electrical, Mechanical, and Firmware subteams, it features a custom designed and configured battery, a battery enclosure, display mounts, and custom display software.

## Features

- Fully electric motorized bike
- Thumb throttle for acceleration
- Touch screen display for speed, distance, and battery tracking
- Custom designed and configured battery
- Battery enclosure
- Display mounts
- Custom display software
