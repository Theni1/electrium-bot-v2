## Description

The team designed and built an electric skateboard from the ground up, integrating mechanical, firmware, and electrical components into a fully functional and efficient board. The work included making the skateboard deck, spot welding and soldering the battery, and integrating firmware into the vehicle.

## Goals

Beyond building a high-performance electric skateboard, the project aimed to foster teamwork and build skills in mechanical design, electrical engineering, and firmware development through hands-on collaboration.
