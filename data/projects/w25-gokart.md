## Description

Electrium's first electric go-kart. The project is unique because it uses two independent electric motors, which adds mechanical and electrical complexity for the team to tackle.

## Features

### Mechanical

Almost everything was designed and manufactured by the team.

### Electrical

Each motor runs at 2000W and 48V. The team worked on purchasing and implementing a BMS with the motors and electronic speed controllers (ESCs), and on voltage control using two buck converters (48V to 12V, and 12V to 5V).

### Firmware

The firmware team works with ESP32 microcontrollers, focused on connecting an OLED display on the steering wheel that shows real-time battery and motor data from the BMS.

## Progress (as of Winter 2025)

- Chassis complete
- Wheel hubs complete
- In progress: brake and pedal mounts, steering wheel, seat, battery box
- The team hoped to have the go-kart driving by the end of the term.
