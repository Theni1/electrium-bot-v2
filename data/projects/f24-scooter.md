## Description

Scooty Barnes is Electrium's electric scooter. It features a CAN bus system for robust communication between electronic components, a custom welded lightweight aluminum deck, and a swing-arm suspension system for smooth riding. A thumb throttle engages the hub motor to accelerate, and disc brakes bring it to a quick stop.
