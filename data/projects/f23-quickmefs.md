## Description

Quickmefs is an electric bike created with funding from MEF (Math Endowment Fund). With these funds, math students helped develop the motor control firmware, which requires complex control theory and motor timing. It features an 8Ah battery using 21700 Samsung cells.

## Specifications

- Battery: 42V/6Ah Lithium-Ion
- Charge time: 3hrs
- Motor: 750W continuous hub motor
- Display: LCD backlit speedometer
- Riding mode: Thumb throttle
- Load capacity: 220lbs
- Range: 20km
- Brakes: Front and back disc brakes
- Wheel: 26-inch fat-tire spoke wheel
- Gear: 8-speed derailleur
- Frame material: Steel
