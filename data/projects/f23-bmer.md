## Description

BMER is an electric bike with a colorful pink-to-white gradient. It has front and rear suspension for bumpier rides, and an 8Ah battery that provides 25% more range than previous vehicles. Many of BMER's parts were later scrapped for use in other projects.

## Specifications

- Battery: 42V/8Ah Lithium-Ion
- Charge time: 3hrs
- Motor: 500W continuous hub motor
- Display: LCD backlit speedometer
- Riding mode: Thumb throttle
- Load capacity: 220lbs
- Range: 30km
- Brakes: Front and back rim brakes
- Wheel: 26-inch spoke wheel
- Gear: 7-speed derailleur
- Frame material: Steel
