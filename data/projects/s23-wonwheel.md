## Description

WonWheel is Electrium's first electric balance board. It features a Spintend 100V100A VESC controller, a 750W stock OneWheel Pint motor, custom lights, and custom machined aluminum railing. It uses an IMU to balance and to detect the direction the rider wants to go. The balance board is a convenient way to travel around campus, with handles for carrying it.

## Specifications

- Battery: 63V/6Ah Lithium-Ion
- Charge time: 3hrs
- Motor: 750W electric go-kart hub motor
- Display: Mobile app
- Riding mode: Balance board
- Load capacity: 220lbs
- Range: 20km
- Frame material: Aluminum channel
