## Description

A custom electric longtail conversion kit that converts an older, unused bike into an electric cargo bike, as an eco-friendly alternative to cars. The original project page describes the features below as planned work.

## Features

### Mechanical

A custom frame designed to mount to a variety of bikes on the market, adding cargo space to a non-cargo bike. CAD, FEA of the frame to verify it supports the intended loads, and manufacturing are all done by the team.

### Electrical and Firmware

A custom 12S4P battery pack and bike computer, both designed and built in house. The pack balances range and power output, and is shaped around the frame geometry to keep the bike nimble despite the added weight. The motor is driven by an ESC configured through the VESC Tool. The custom bike computer shows telemetry from the ESC and sensors (over UART, I2C, and SPI) and includes Google Maps integration.
