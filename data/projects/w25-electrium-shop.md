## Description

The Electrium Mobility Shop is an online storefront to showcase and sell Electrium's personal electric vehicles and accessories. Customers can browse products, view detailed descriptions, and make purchases. It is aimed at students, sponsors, and the broader electric mobility community.

## Features

- Product listings: Electric mobility products (e-bikes, skateboards, and OneWheels) with images, descriptions, and prices.
- Product pages: A dedicated page per product with images, pricing, specifications, and availability.
- Cart and checkout: Add items to a cart and check out.
- Stock management: Real-time stock availability for each product.
- Rentals (planned): A future section for renting electric mobility devices for a set period.
- Responsive design: Works on desktop and mobile.
