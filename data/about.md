## Overview

Electrium Mobility is a student-led design team at the University of Waterloo that designs and builds personal electric vehicles (PEVs), such as electric bikes, skateboards, scooters, OneWheels, and other portable electric mobility options. Its goal is to create sustainable and affordable transportation in the form of personal electric vehicles, and to promote their adoption.

The team is part of the Sedra Student Design Centre and Waterloo Engineering, and is listed under the Automotives & Transportation and Sustainable Energy categories of Waterloo's student design team directory.

The team prioritizes being beginner-friendly: it aims to give students hands-on experience while minimizing the learning curve. It suits students who want to prototype quickly, learn from mistakes, and contribute to sustainability. The team has held internal competitions where sub-teams test and compete with the vehicles they have built.

## Mission and Values

The team aims to inspire students, build awareness, and advocate for the economic and environmental benefits of electric mobility.

- Sustainability: Raising awareness of sustainable vehicle technology and greener transportation choices.
- Affordability: Promoting the economic benefits of electric vehicles for students.
- Portability: Creating compact, convenient electric vehicles for efficient commuting.
- Student learning: Teaching students to design and build electric vehicles.

## Team Structure

Projects are built by subteams covering mechanical (frames, CAD, manufacturing), electrical (battery packs, PCBs, power systems), and firmware (microcontrollers, displays, motor control). The team also has web development and software projects, such as the Electrium Shop and Discord bots, and a management team.

Each term, the team runs several projects in parallel, each with a project lead. Terms follow Waterloo's calendar: Winter (January to April), Spring (May to August), and Fall (September to December).

## Joining the Team

Joining has three steps:

1. Discover Electrium: Learn about current projects and connect through social media.
2. Apply: Submit an application describing yourself and why you're interested. Applications are posted on the website's Join Our Team page (https://electriummobility.com/join-our-team) when open.
3. Interview: Chat with the team, meet members, and ask questions.

No prior experience is required. Members of all skill levels are welcome and receive learning materials, mentorship, and guidance. Members work on electrical components, firmware, and the mechanical structure of vehicles.
